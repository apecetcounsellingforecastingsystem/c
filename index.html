About the Platform

Democratizing Lateral Entry Admissions in Andhra Pradesh

The Andhra Pradesh Engineering Common Entrance Test (AP ECET) is conducted by the Department of Technical Education and APSCHE for Diploma holders in Engineering & Technology and B.Sc. (Mathematics) degree holders for lateral entry admission into the 2nd year of regular B.E. / B.Tech courses.

Due to multi-round web counseling dynamics, regional quota reservations (Andhra University vs. Sri Venkateswara University area), and annual cutoff rank fluctuations, candidates frequently struggle to identify their genuine chances of admission into top autonomous institutions.

📈

Multi-Year Ingestion

Ingests real closing rank datasets across 2023 and 2024 with normalized 2025–2026 predictions.

⚖️

Local vs. Non-Local Quota

Applies the statutory 85% local candidate quota and 15% open merit unreserved (UR) calculation rules.

🛡️

Zero Bias Analytics

Objective mathematical modeling ensuring students maximize their chances at government and autonomous colleges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are cutoff ranks forecasted for 2026? ▼
Our hybrid model performs weighted trend regression on verified 2023 and 2024 closing ranks while factoring in branch demand shifts and seat capacity changes.
What do Safe, Moderate, and Dream labels mean? ▼
Safe means your rank is well within previous closing marks (>80% odds). Moderate indicates competitive contention (50-80%). Ambitious and Dream reflect reach options with closing ranks lower than your score.
Can I export my option list directly for APSCHE? ▼
Yes! Once logged in, you can add choices to your Preference List, arrange the order via drag-and-drop, and generate a printable PDF or CSV file.
