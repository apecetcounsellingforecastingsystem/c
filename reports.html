🎓 Viva Reference & Architectural Explanation

1. Hybrid Design

Linear Regression models linear drift; Decision Trees capture multi-branch non-linear cutoff partitions; Random Forest combines 35+ ensemble estimators to eliminate overfitting and minimize variance.

2. Recency Weighting

2024 historical cutoffs receive highest weighting (0.45), 2023 (0.25), and 2025-2026 projected cutoffs (0.30) to preserve current post-pandemic lateral entry patterns.

3. Explainable AI

Rather than returning a purely black-box score, every forecast delivers actionable natural language justifications analyzing candidate rank margins, seat trends, and regional quotas.
